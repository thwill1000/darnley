# LOC019_SUMMER_HOUSE

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no characters, no UI elements, no text overlays.

A Victorian octagonal summer house, positioned slightly off-centre within the composition rather than dead-centre, on a patch of snow-covered lawn. The focal point is the structure itself - an ornate eight-sided wooden garden building with a steeply pitched, faceted tent-like roof rising to a small finial or weathervane at its peak. Each visible facet of the octagon is framed in dark timber with decorative fretwork or trellis-style panelling between slender posts, giving a gazebo-like delicacy rather than a plain shed. Tall narrow windows with small glazing bars occupy alternate facets, glowing pale and warm from brighter light within, in contrast to the muted grey daylight outside. A single open entrance faces the viewer, recessed and visible as a dark doorway flanked by ornamental woodwork. Through the doorway and windows the interior is clearly visible: croquet mallets and hoops as angular shapes, a scatter of tiny pale cigarette ends on the floor, and a small pale folded handkerchief lying near the threshold. The second anchor is two distinct tracks of footprints in the snow leading to and from the entrance only, approaching from different directions. From the left foreground, a track of heavy hobnailed men's bootprints; from the right, a separate track of smaller, narrower lady's shoeprints. The two tracks converge on the entrance, each showing several overlapping passes back and forth rather than a single clean line. The rest of the snow around the building lies smooth and undisturbed, with patches of withered grass showing through away from the building. The background shows distant trees or shrubs rendered as dark silhouettes. The timber shows weathering and grain texture suggested through subtle lines, with the structure raised slightly on a low decorative plinth or step. Colour palette: dark browns and faded green-painted trim for the woodwork, warm pale glow at the windows and doorway, cream and grey snow, pale overcast sky. Cold, somewhat isolated, quaintly ornamental feeling, with an inviting warmth glimpsed within. Winter daylight. No figures.
