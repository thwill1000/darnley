# LOC030_WEST_GUEST_ROOM

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no characters, no UI elements, no text overlays.

A guest room stripped for redecoration, bare and half-finished. The focal point is the long wall opposite the viewer, from which the old wallpaper has been peeled away in places: pale grey-cream bare plaster shows in uneven patches, and long ragged strips of faded green floral paper still hang from the upper wall and curl at their ends, rendered as simple vertical shapes with a few darker pattern flecks. A wooden stepladder leans against the stripped wall on the left, and beside it stands a plain wooden pasting table stacked with exactly four rolls of new wallpaper, rendered as pale cylinders, with a paste bucket and brush on the table's end. Two paint pots stand on the floor near the table. Exactly two shapes covered by pale dust sheets stand against the far wall, draped in soft angular folds so that nothing beneath them can be identified - no bed, no wardrobe, no other furniture is visible. The floorboards are bare, rendered with linear perspective and a thick, even film of pale dust; the dust is smooth and unmarked, with no footprints, drag marks or trails of any kind. A tall sash window in the right wall lets in dim grey winter daylight, with a few strips of dust sheet or newspaper taped across its lower panes. A single dark wood door stands closed in the near left wall. The ceiling is plain pale plaster, slightly stained at one corner. Colour palette: pale grey-cream plaster, faded green wallpaper, light wood for ladder and table, off-white dust sheets, dusty grey-brown floorboards, muted cool window light. Cold, empty, unfinished atmosphere. Dim interior daylight. No figures.
