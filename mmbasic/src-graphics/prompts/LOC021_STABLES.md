# LOC021_STABLES

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no UI elements, no text overlays.

Stable block complex with mixed conditions. The focal point is a two-part structure: one side shows traditional stable doors and windows with loose slates visible on the roof (rendered as dark irregular shapes), the other side converted to a garage with a large Daimler barely visible beneath a worn tarpaulin (suggested as pale draped form). Small dark oil stains mark the snow near the car. The stable yard between buildings is rendered with snow and scattered straw. A loose box shows a bay horse's head briefly visible through an opening—rendered as a simple warm brown shape. Tucked into a sheltered doorway at the edge of the frame, a sizeable ginger cat sits watchfully in the straw—larger and heavier-set than an ordinary cat but proportioned so as to remain clearly a cat, not a horse—rendered as a small, secondary detail rather than a focal point, a bold rounded orange-and-white shape with a few darker tabby lines. Tack room door is suggested. Walls are weathered brick in muted brown-reds; paint flakes are suggested through texture. Colour palette: weathered brick reds, dark roof slates, pale tarpaulin, brown wood doors, pale snow, small dark oil stains, warm ginger-orange for the cat. Cold, slightly dingy feeling. No human figures.
