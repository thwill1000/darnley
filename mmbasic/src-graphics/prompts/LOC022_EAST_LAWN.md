# LOC022_EAST_LAWN

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no characters, no UI elements, no text overlays.

Open lawn lying between the stable block and the gamekeeper's cottage. The focal point is a single trail of deep, purposeful hobnailed boot prints running between the two - from the stable roofs just visible at the left edge to the small dark cottage under chestnut trees at the distant right - rendered as clear dark lines suggesting serious, hurried movement, going and returning, slightly offset. A dark line of box hedge in the background defines the northern boundary. The foreground snow is largely undisturbed at the margins but broken by the trail in the centre. A single rook picking in the middle of the lawn is rendered as a tiny dark shape. Sky is pale grey, overcast. Colour palette: cream and grey snow, dark boot prints, dark hedge silhouette, muted sky. Cold, open, somewhat desolate. Winter daylight. No human figures.
