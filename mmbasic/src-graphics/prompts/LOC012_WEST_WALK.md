# LOC012_WEST_WALK

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no characters, no UI elements, no text overlays.

A snow-covered tennis court fills the left foreground, its sagging net and boundary markings rendered as simple pale lines across the snow. Beside it a gravel path runs clockwise around the house, rendered as a diagonal line of pale grey gravel receding toward the horizon. Several overlapping sets of footprints run back and forth along the path, visible as darker shadowed indentations: broad flat-footed bootprints, slipper prints and men’s shoeprints heading toward the front of the house. Separately, a lighter trail of small lady’s shoeprints leaves the path and crosses the snow beside the court toward the distance. To the right, a bed of withered flowers shows dark woody stems and branches poking through patches of snow. The background shows the distant corner of the house rendered as a dark silhouette. Snow patches are irregular, thicker in shadowed areas. Colour palette: pale grey gravel, cream snow, dark browns for withered plants and distant stonework, pale sky. No figure. Winter daylight, flat and cold.
