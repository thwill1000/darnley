# LOC008_HALL

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no characters, no UI elements, no text overlays.

Grand entrance hall with high ceiling and marble floor. The dominant focal point is a broad oak staircase ascending to the right-rear, rendered with bold geometric steps and dark wood railings—perspective emphasizes recession. Two suits of armour stand sentinel on either side of the staircase base, rendered as dark metal silhouettes with visible breastplate reflections. The floor is black-and-white marble in a simple geometric pattern. A heavy oak front door with brass fittings occupies the left foreground, with a damp coir doormat bearing faint muddy impressions. A modest display case is visible on the wall.

A doorway in the left-hand wall, beyond the front door, is almost completely
blocked by a tall, untidy stack of household furniture - upright chairs, a
small table tipped on its side, a rolled rug and a bedside cabinet, partly
covered by a pale dust sheet - piled against the door so that only a strip of
its dark frame is visible. The stack is rendered as a cluttered mass of dark
wood and angular shapes. Nothing else in the hall is stacked or cluttered.

The ceiling is high and dark, suggested by minimal cross-hatching. Walls are cream with shadows indicating wood panelling, hung with a few crossed swords
and a pair of long Civil War pikes mounted upright, rendered as thin dark vertical and diagonal lines against the cream.

Colour palette: marble blacks and creams, dark wood browns, brass highlights, pale cream walls. No figures. Dim interior lighting, cool daylight from the fanlight above the door.
