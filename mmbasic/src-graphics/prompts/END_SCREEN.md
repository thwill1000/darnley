# END_SCREEN

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 640x480 pixels (displayed at 320x240), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. This is an ending/outro screen, not a gameplay location.

The viewpoint is positioned on the drive beyond the park gates, looking away from Darnley Manor entirely — the house, summer house, and outbuildings are all behind the viewer and none of them appear anywhere in the frame. The focal point, in the foreground and just left of centre, is the rear of an old black 1920s police saloon — round tail lamp, spare wheel mounted flat on the back, folded canvas roof, and dark rear window — receding away down a snow-dusted country lane, having just passed through the gates. The car is angled slightly so both the rear and one side are readable, its wheels leaving fresh dark tyre tracks pressed into the thin snow behind it.

The wrought-iron park gates and flanking stone gate-piers stand in the near middle distance behind the car, now being left behind, with a short run of the estate's high stone wall visible to one side before it gives way to a low hedge bordering open countryside. The lane continues away from the viewer into soft winter haze. Snow-covered fields and hedgerows spread either side of the lane, with scattered bare winter trees along the field boundaries. Rising above the distant tree-line, roughly centred toward the horizon, the slender stone steeple of a village church is just visible — a small, simple silhouette pointing into the pale sky, suggesting the nearby village and the journey's true destination.

No figures are visible; the departure is conveyed by the empty lane and the car alone.

Title text is rendered as part of the scene, not a separate overlay: bold blocky pixel-art lettering reading "THE END" is positioned in the upper portion of the image against the sky, in the same dark serif-inspired pixel font with hard, unaliased edges used on the game's splash screen, coloured a deep dried-blood red with a thin black pixel outline for legibility against the pale cloud. No subtitle line beneath it.

Colour palette: deep black for the departing car with a single warm highlight on its tail lamp glass, pale gravel and grey-white snow, dark iron gates and stone wall, muted winter hedgerows and trees, a pale grey stone church steeple against the sky, deep red and pale grey-white for the title text, flat overcast sky fading to a slightly warmer, paler tone near the horizon suggesting the close of the day. Cold, quiet, conclusive winter daylight — a sense of departure and resolution. No figures.
