# LOC018_EAST_WALK

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no characters, no UI elements, no text overlays.

Gravel path running east-west across the front of the house, flanked by an avenue of statues. The focal point is the central perspective of the path receding toward the horizon, rendered as converging pale grey gravel lines with snow accumulation along the edges. The red-brick corner and front wall of the manor run along the left edge of the frame. Statues line the path on both sides, rendered as simple dark stone silhouettes alternating left-right — simplified as pedestaled forms with minimal detail. Two sets of footprints mark the snow-covered gravel: broad flat-footed bootprints and smaller slipper prints, overlapping, running along the path and curving around the corner of the house toward the far end. No other prints. At the far end the path meets a dark line of low box hedge (the maze), and off to the right, a small dark cottage roof is faintly suggested among bare chestnut trees in the winter haze. Sky is pale and overcast. Colour palette: pale grey gravel, cream snow, dark stone statues and pedestals, red-brown brick, pale grey-blue sky. Cold, formal atmosphere. Winter daylight, clear and flat. No figures.
