# LOC017_DRIVE

Pixel art, VGA 256-colour palette, 480x320 pixels (displayed at 240x160), style of early 1990s point-and-click adventure games such as Police Quest or King's Quest VI. Hard pixel edges throughout, no anti-aliasing, no modern rendering techniques. Muted period-appropriate colour palette. Static background scene, no UI elements, no text overlays.

Looking through open wrought-iron park gates, down a gravel drive dusted with snow that curves gently toward a small red-brick Georgian manor house, larger and more prominent than a distant speck — its full facade clearly readable even from across the parkland. A confused tangle of footprints criss-crosses the snow of the drive in all directions, thickest near the front steps. A high stone wall flanks the gates. A 1920s police constable stands by one gate-pier, rendered with clear detail: navy tunic with a row of buttons, a wide leather belt, dark trousers, and the distinctive domed custodian helmet with a small badge visible at the front; his pose is upright and watchful, hands loosely clasped behind his back. Snow-covered parkland spreads either side, largely open, with only a few bare winter trees scattered well back toward the tree-line rather than close to the viewer; to the left the open West lawn lies in front of a gravel path running west. Far left, barely visible against the tree-line, a small octagonal Victorian summer house. An old black 1920s police saloon — round headlamps, running boards, spare wheel — is parked on the drive close to the house. Stone lions and steps mark the entrance; the symmetrical brick facade shows two sash windows on each side of the central pedimented front door, evenly spaced across the ground floor, with a matching row of windows above. To the right of the house a gravel path runs off along the front of the house between an avenue of statues on plinths, spaced at intervals and rendered as simple pale stone silhouettes; beyond the avenue a yard gate in a low wall opens toward the low roofs of a stable block, kept well below the house's own roofline. To the left of the house, a snow-covered tennis court is faintly suggested by the line of its net and boundary markings. Colour palette: red brick, cream trim, pale gravel, grey-white snow, navy constable's uniform, dark iron and trees, muted overcast sky. Flat winter daylight. No other figures.
